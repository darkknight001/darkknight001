### 🧭 About me

Backend engineer and tech lead with about six years of Python, across FinTech and EdTech.
I build distributed systems that stay up: microservices, event-driven pipelines,
high-availability databases, observability, and LLM features that run in production.

### ⚡ What I'm good at

- 🚀 **Backend & APIs.** High-traffic REST services in Python with FastAPI and Django, built for low latency and high uptime.
- 🧩 **Distributed systems.** Microservices, event-driven design with Kafka, async workers and queues, and multi-tenant architectures.
- 🛡️ **High availability.** PostgreSQL clustering and failover with Patroni and etcd, multi-AZ deployments, and removing single points of failure.
- 📈 **Observability.** Metrics, logs and dashboards with ClickHouse, Grafana, Prometheus and ELK, plus incident response.
- ☁️ **Cloud & platform.** AWS, Kubernetes (EKS), Helm, Docker, CI/CD, and hybrid or on-prem deployments.
- 🤖 **GenAI.** LLM applications, RAG pipelines, embeddings and vector search with LangChain and pgvector, shipped to production.
- 🧭 **Tech leadership.** Architecture reviews, system design, and mentoring engineers.
